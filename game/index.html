Pirul to Pellet

Every summer the chir pine forests of Kumaon drop a thick carpet of dry needles, called pirul. Left on the ground it feeds forest fires. Collected, it becomes fuel pellets. Keep the forest floor clear and keep the plant running.

  1. Rake fallen pirul in the forest by walking over it. It fills your doko basket.
  2. Empty the doko at the baling press. Every 20 kg is pressed into one bundle.
  3. The truck carries bundles to the raw material shed. It leaves when full, or send it early.
  4. Pick bundles up at the shed and carry them to the pellet plant hopper.
  5. The plant presses pellets. Each bundle makes 17 kg of pellets, sold at ₹9 a kg.

Tap or click where you want to walk, or use the arrow keys or WASD. Spend your earnings on upgrades and hired help. If the fire risk reaches 100%, the forest catches fire and the season ends.
