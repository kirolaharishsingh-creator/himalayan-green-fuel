Who we are

Clean energy from the Kumaon hills

Himalayan Green Fuel LLP makes biomass pellets from fallen chir pine needles at our plant in Someshwar, Almora.

Women from local Self Help Groups collect the needles and earn an income of their own, while the forest floor is cleared of fire fuel.

Reducing forest fires. Delivering renewable fuel.

2
The problem

Dry needles. Burning forests.

Every summer, fallen chir pine needles carpet the forest floor across Uttarakhand. They are dry, oily and catch fire easily, feeding wildfires that damage forests, wildlife and villages.

Pine needles are one of the main fuels behind forest fires in the hills.

At the same time, hill villages have few local jobs, and women rarely have an income of their own.

We saw a fire risk and turned it into a resource.
